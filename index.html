About me

I am an Application Scientist at Cusp AI in Singapore, with a background in computational materials science and an interest in applying artificial intelligence to scientific discovery. My work connects atomistic simulation, machine learning, and materials chemistry to help translate scientific methods into useful applications.

Before moving into industry, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST) and Imperial College London. I earned my PhD in Materials Research at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Aron Walsh. My doctoral research focused on the structural dynamics, phase stability, and disorder of metal halide perovskites, using machine-learning interatomic potentials and large-scale molecular dynamics.

I develop computational workflows that combine density functional theory, machine-learning force fields, molecular dynamics, and Python-based structural analysis. I am particularly interested in scalable, data-driven methods for understanding complex materials and enabling materials discovery.

Kinetically Arrested Twin-Domain State in Formamidinium Lead Iodide

Xia Liang, Milos Dubajic, Zezhu Zeng, Yang Lu, Johan Klarbring, Samuel D. Stranks, Aron Walsh
J. Am. Chem. Soc. 148, 35995–36005 (2026)

Large-scale machine-learning molecular dynamics reveals a history-dependent, kinetically arrested twin-domain state in formamidinium lead iodide (FAPbI₃). Coupled octahedral tilting and molecular reorientation govern the emergence of this low-temperature structure, with signatures in diffuse scattering, vibrational response and electronic disorder.

Phase Stability and Transformations in Lead Mixed Halide Perovskites from Machine Learning Force Fields

Xia Liang, Johan Klarbring, Aron Walsh
Chem. Mater. 37, 9392–9405 (2025)

We combine on-the-fly machine-learning force-field development, large-scale molecular dynamics and PDynA structural analysis to map phase stability in mixed-halide perovskites. The results show how A-site cations, halide composition and local segregation reshape octahedral-tilt correlations and phase-transformation pathways.

Structural Dynamics Descriptors for Metal Halide Perovskites

Xia Liang, Johan Klarbring, William J. Baldwin, Zhenzhu Li, Gábor Csányi, Aron Walsh
J. Phys. Chem. C 127, 19141–19151 (2023)

This work introduces quantitative descriptors and the Python-based PDynA framework for analysing structural dynamics in metal halide perovskites. By tracking lattice distortions, octahedral tilts, molecular orientations and spatial correlations, it connects atomistic trajectories to interpretable patterns of local order and disorder.
